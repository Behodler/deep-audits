## A. Original finding record (audit 20) — **reconstructed from the finding JSON, not from a published report**

**Title:** WATCH-19 re-derivation: one of the two premises the nudge design leans on no longer holds, and the other is empirical rather than structural

**Root cause class:** `honeypot-safety-premises-not-established-by-construction`

**Description:** §1 claims the pot is *"by construction a fraction of the cost of the `nudgeSize` mints"* and that *"there is no configuration of this mechanism under which claiming is profitable-in-isolation"*. **Neither is established by construction.**

**Attack path (as recorded):**

1. §1 claims the pot is "by construction a fraction of the cost of the `nudgeSize` mints" and that "there is no configuration of this mechanism under which claiming is profitable-in-isolation".
2. Neither is established by construction. `BatchNFTMinter.sol:57-59`'s "by construction" is backed by **no code** (the whole file was read to check); executed counterexamples run at **9.5×** and **20,000×**.
3. The observed margin is ~**6×** (88 USDC pot vs ~518 USDS for 40 mints) — **empirical, not structural**.
4. A prolonged period of no batching (bots offline, gas spike, UI outage) lets the pot cross the profitability line with **no on-chain brake**.

**Impact:** No standalone loss. This is the **amplifier** that sets the ceiling on the nudge-lineage findings, and it removes the safety argument the design document leans on.

**Likelihood:** low as a standalone event; **certain as an amplifier** of the two Mediums.

**Assumptions / external requirements:** none — this is a documentation-vs-code discrepancy verified from source.

**Severity justification (verbatim from the record):** Low. No standalone loss and no independent attack path — the value impact is entirely accounted for in the nudge-lineage findings, so raising this above Low would double-count. It is filed rather than folded silently into the existing suppressions because it **is** the `WATCH-19-nudge-law1-reopen-trigger` firing on its own terms, and because **suppressing a finding under the very lineage whose premises it falsifies is exactly the circularity Law 1 forbids.**

**Recommendation:** Rewrite §1 to state the margin as an **operational** invariant that must be monitored, not a structural one — or enforce it in code (cap the payout at a value-based fraction of what the batch actually paid).

**Watch-note consequence:** `WATCH-19-nudge-law1-reopen-trigger` upgraded from **DEGRADED** to **FIRED-AND-WITNESSED**. Both disjuncts of the note's own wording are satisfied and both are executed.

**PoC:** no dedicated file. Executed counterexamples at 9.5× and 20,000× (`PoC_NudgeLineage_H01` / `_NudgeDrain`, 7/7 PASS @ `0d1a0b2`).

### The actual behaviour at HEAD

**No code enforces any ratio, cap, or value comparison.** Both files were read in full, plus a
non-truncated grep of every comparison operator. In `src/BatchNFTMinterMultiToken.sol` every
comparison is one of:

- `:352` — a **count** gate (`count >= nudgeSize`), purely numeric;
- `:363` / `:424` / `:454` — loop bounds;
- `:382` / `:384` — payment-token dust and refund arithmetic;
- `:431` — `available < minReward`, a **caller**-protective minimum, never a protocol-protective maximum;
- `:456` — a zero skip.

`paymentAmount` (`:297`, `:357`, `:384`, `:386`) **never shares an expression** with `snapshot`,
`available`, or any reward amount, and the contract never reads the dispatcher's price at all (only
`primeToken()` at `:322`). The frozen sibling is identical in conclusion.

The claim is therefore an **off-chain funding-discipline property presented as a structural one**.

The document concedes as much three lines later, at `docs/multi-token-nudge.md:48-50`:

> ```
> If someone erroneously over-funds the contract beyond the mint cost and a bot
> snipes it, that is still correct behaviour — the error was in the sender, not in
> `BatchNFTMinterMultiToken`.
> ```

That sentence and "there is no configuration of this mechanism under which claiming is
profitable-in-isolation" cannot both be true. And the claim is falsifiable from **owner-reachable
state alone**: `setNudgeSize` (`src/BatchNFTMinterMultiToken.sol:166-169`) has **no lower bound**, so
`nudgeSize = 1` makes a single mint qualify for the entire balance of every listed token, while the
pot side is permissionless and unbounded.

**structurally** rather than by configuration.
