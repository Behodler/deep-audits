## Finding description and impact

### Summary

`PhlimboV3.pauseWithdraw` ([PhlimboV3.sol#L596-L611](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L596-L611)) reduces `totalStaked` at [:602](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L602) but never calls `_updatePhUSDEmissionRate()` ([:893-901](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L893-L901)). It is the **only** `totalStaked`-mutating site that skips the re-derivation:

| Site | Mutation | Re-derives rate? |
|---|---|---|
| `stake` | `totalStaked += amount` (`:668`) | Yes — `:674` |
| `withdraw` | `totalStaked -= actualWithdrawAmount` (`:714`) | Yes — `:725` |
| `setDesiredAPY` (commit branch) | — | Yes — `:245` |
| **`pauseWithdraw`** | **`totalStaked -= amount` (`:602`)** | **No** |

`phUSDPerSecond` therefore stays anchored to the pre-exit `totalStaked` (S0), while `_updatePool` divides that stale numerator by the post-exit `totalStaked` (S1) at [:805](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L805). Surviving stakers realize the departed cohort's entire emission.

### Prior art — why this is re-filed

**This root cause has been dispositioned before, and the reader is entitled to know that before reading the rest.** Two ledger entries cover it:

| Ledger entry | Contract | Status | Owner rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M-05** (`af458c9c`) | `src/Phlimbo.sol` (V1) | acknowledged | *"V1 deprecated — all users migrated to PhlimboV2."* Carries a `v2Disposition: "CARRIES-INTO-V2"`. |
| **V2-M-03** (`b99e8023`) | `src/PhlimboV2.sol` | **wont-fix** | *"This is intended behaviour. It's an implicit penalty to encourage users to not use this unless it is absolutely necessary."* |

The re-file basis is **not** a fingerprint change. It is four specific facts:

1. **Different contract.** This reports `src/PhlimboV3.sol`, new in stories 024–028. V2-M-03 is scoped to `src/PhlimboV2.sol:280-291`; the defect has never been dispositioned on V3.

2. **The owner deliberately reworked this exact function in-range, and fixed a different bug in it.** V3's `pauseWithdraw` adds a debt realignment at [:604-606](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L604-L606) whose own docblock at [:586-591](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L586-L591) states it exists to fix the latent V1/V2 underflow brick. The owner had this function open, made a V3 delta to it, and left the rate re-derivation out. That is the substantive basis for re-filing: this is a fresh authoring decision on fresh code, not a re-litigation of a closed one.

3. **The penalty rationale cannot cover this harm — it describes a different party.** *"Implicit penalty"* is a coherent and legitimate design intent for what the **exiter** gives up. But the over-mint's harm does not land on the exiter. It lands on **surviving stakers** (who receive the unbacked phUSD) and on **all phUSD holders** (who absorb the dilution) — parties who took no action, chose no penalty, and had no notice. Penalty semantics govern the penalized party's forfeit; they cannot license harm to third parties. This is the argument, stated plainly, so the owner can reject it if they disagree.

4. **V3 makes the pause routine, which the penalty rationale does not presume.** The rationale reads as premised on `pauseWithdraw` being an emergency exit used rarely ("unless it is absolutely necessary"). In V3, `beginFlush` calls `_pause()` at [:433](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L433) on **every** promo rotation, so the pause window is scheduled operations, not an emergency. And in that window the penalty measures **zero** — see *Two distinct quantities* below.

**This is also NOT ledger DEDUP-04.** DEDUP-04 (`phlimbo-v3:...:uncapped-unbacked-phUSD-mint-config-conditional`, suppressed) is the *designed* mint-on-demand solvency model per `SolvencyDetermination.md` §2. This finding is emission **exceeding the design's own APY rate** — an accounting defect in the rate, not the design. DEDUP-04's own suppression block lists V2-M-03 as *"[CONTEXT ONLY, NOT LOAD-BEARING]"*. A future run must not collapse the two.

### Two distinct quantities — the exiter's forfeit vs. the leak

These are separate numbers and the report keeps them separate:

- **Forfeit of already-accrued rewards** — what `:604-606` takes from the exiter by realigning debts to the reduced amount. In the routine-rotation path this is **exactly zero**, because `beginFlush` calls `_updatePool()` at [:432](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L432) immediately before `_pause()` at `:433`: everything accrued is already settled into the accumulator, so there is nothing left to forfeit. The "implicit penalty" is not collected in the window where the leak occurs.
- **Forgone future accrual** — what the exiter gives up by not being staked during the window. This equals the leak, and it is what makes the self-serving variant self-defeating (below). It is not a penalty the protocol imposes; it is the ordinary consequence of not being staked.

### The asymmetry is the finding

The two reward legs in `_updatePool` are not guarded alike:

```solidity
// Stable leg — CAPPED by rewardBalance (:786-795)
uint256 potentialReward = (rewardPerSecond * timeElapsed) / PRECISION;
// Cap distribution by rewardBalance to prevent over-distribution
uint256 toDistribute = potentialReward > rewardBalance ? rewardBalance : potentialReward;

// phUSD leg — NO CAP (:803-806)
if (phUSDPerSecond > 0) {
    uint256 phUSDReward = timeElapsed * phUSDPerSecond;
    accPhUSDPerShare += (phUSDReward * PRECISION) / totalStaked;
}
```

On the stable leg a stale rate is bounded by `rewardBalance` — the consequence is mere redistribution of tokens the protocol already holds. The phUSD leg has no such ceiling: the excess is minted at [:853](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L853):

```solidity
uint256 pendingPhUSDAmount = (userDetails.amount * accPhUSDPerShare) / PRECISION - userDetails.phUSDDebt;
if (pendingPhUSDAmount > 0) {
    phUSD.mint(beneficiary, pendingPhUSDAmount);
}
```

**To be precise about what is and is not novel here:** minting phUSD rewards against no collateral inflow is the **designed** model, not the defect. `_claimRewards:853` mints unbacked by construction, and the entire `desiredAPYBps` stream is unbacked-by-construction via `_updatePhUSDEmissionRate` at [:899](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L899) — that is `SolvencyDetermination.md` §2 and ledger DEDUP-04, not this finding. The defect is that the stale rate makes the protocol **over-pay its own APY stream** during the window: the same stream, at up to double the intended total, distributed to the wrong set of holders. The mint is the delivery mechanism, not the novelty.

### Reachability — no attacker, no owner error, at any step

All steps verified in source at `02b9bc2`:

1. **The pause is routine, not exceptional.** `beginFlush` calls `_pause()` at [:433](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L433), so the pool is paused on **every** promo rotation — not only in emergencies.
2. **The exit window is opened by the pause itself.** `pauseWithdraw` is `whenPaused`, so every flush window is an exit window. The function's own docblock at [:592-594](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L592-L594) explicitly contemplates a full exit during a flush.
3. **The stale rate can be cemented by anyone.** `collectReward` ([:618](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L618)) is `external nonReentrant` — **permissionless and not `whenNotPaused`** — and calls `_updatePool()` at [:622](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L622). Any address can poke it with 1 wei and bake the stale rate into `accPhUSDPerShare`. That accumulator only ever rises, so a later `stake`/`withdraw` repairs the **rate** but never claws back the accumulator.

This is not a Law-3 admin-mistake finding: no owner misconfiguration occurs at any step. The trigger is the intersection of two ordinary operations — a scheduled rotation and any staker exiting.

### Impact — measured, and bounded

At the real operating point (68,000 phUSD staked against ~68,212 live supply, 5% APY per the docstring at [:63](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L63), 1-day flush window, a whale exiting 60,000 of 68,000):

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| phUSD minted to survivor | `9.315068493150632` |
| Fair APY entitlement | `1.095890410958832` |
| **Excess over APY budget** | **`8.219178082191800` phUSD/day** |
| Over-mint factor (S0/S1) | `8.5x` |
| Share of live supply | ~0.012%/day |

The two numbers must be read together. The closed form is:

```
excess == exitedAmount × APY × window / year
```

which the PoC reproduces to within 1e12 wei (`60,000 × 5% ÷ 365 = 8.219178082191780821` predicted vs `8.219178082191727891` measured). **Principal is never at risk.**

### Why this is not High

The escalation was considered and refuted. It rests on reading the `8.5x` factor as the impact, and the factor is the wrong number:

- **The closed form contains no S1 term.** The factor `S0/S1` is unbounded as `S1 → 0`, but driving S1 to dust does **not** enlarge the absolute leak — it concentrates the same rate-limited leak on fewer survivors. The leak is emission-rate-limited, not principal-scaled.
- **The over-mint is bounded by the protocol's own pre-existing budget.** This is the tightest bound available and it is decisive. The stale rate is `S0 × APY / year` — *exactly the rate the protocol was already emitting the second before the exit*. The failure is that the rate does not come **down** when stake leaves. So the worst case is: **the protocol keeps paying the APY it was already paying, for the length of the window.** Total emission over the window never exceeds what the owner had already committed to at S0. That is a bounded failure-to-reduce an existing obligation, not a novel solvency hole, and it cannot exceed 2x the intended emission for the window.
- **It does not compound across rotations.** Each window's leak is independent and additive, not multiplicative. `_updatePool` early-returns when `totalStaked == 0` ([:779](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L779)), and every window's stale rate is repaired at the first post-unpause `stake` ([:674](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L674)) or `withdraw` ([:725](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L725)). Rotations are separately bounded; there is no escalating ratchet.
- **Nothing is stolen.** No attacker profits and no existing balance is taken.
- **The self-serving variant is never profitable.** An exiter splitting across two addresses (A exits, B stays) nets `leak × (B/S1 − 1)`, which is **≤ 0** for all configurations — and equals zero only in the degenerate case where B is the sole survivor. They forgo future accrual on the exited stake equal to the leak they create, then share that leak with honest survivors. The payoff is never positive, so there is no attacker incentive to construct.
- At real parameters the leak is ~0.012%/day of supply — orders of magnitude from the "assets can be stolen/lost" bar.

### Why this is not Low

Low ("state handling") is the natural reading of a missing function call, but it understates the harm:

- **The state error is not the harm; the over-emission is.** The consequence is `phUSD.mint()` issuing tokens **in excess of the design's own APY rate** — a quantified value leak against a stated budget, not bookkeeping. (The mint being unbacked is the design; the mint exceeding the rate is the bug. See the asymmetry section.)
- **The owner's own code argues against Low.** The stable leg is deliberately capped at `:789` with the comment *"Cap distribution by rewardBalance to prevent over-distribution"*. The owner already treats uncapped over-distribution as a defect worth guarding — and the one leg with no such ceiling is the one left unguarded.
- **Third parties absorb the loss.** The exiter is not the beneficiary. The excess phUSD lands on surviving stakers and the dilution lands on all phUSD holders — parties who took no action and had no notice. This is why the standing *"implicit penalty"* rationale (ledger V2-M-03, see *Prior art* above) does not reach this harm: it describes the exiter's forfeit at `:604-606`, which is real, implemented, measures **zero** in the routine-rotation window, and is not what this reports. Penalty semantics cannot justify harm landing on third parties.

This is the C4 Medium clause verbatim: value leaks, assets are not at direct risk, assumptions are stated, external requirements are documented.

### Stated assumption — dormant is not invalid

**The finding turns entirely on the deployed `desiredAPYBps`. Zero APY fully immunizes it**: the phUSD leg is gated on `phUSDPerSecond > 0` at `:803`, and `test_A_zeroAPYImmunizes` confirms no mint occurs at all under zero APY.

**The construction default is knowable, and it is zero.** `desiredAPYBps` is declared at [:63](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L63) and has **no constructor initializer** — the constructor at [:210-226](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L210-L226) sets `phUSD`, `rewardToken`, `depletionDuration`, `lastRewardTime`, `rewardBalance` and `rewardPerSecond`, and never touches `desiredAPYBps`. It is therefore **0 by construction**, and the only path that arms it is the two-step `setDesiredAPY` at [:233-248](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L233-L248). So the accurate statement is: **dormant at deploy, armed only by a deliberate owner action — an action whose UI gives no warning that it activates a mint leak.**

Both halves matter. The construction default settles what a fresh deployment does. The **live** `desiredAPYBps` of an already-deployed instance is a chain read and cannot be settled from source — an owner may already have committed a non-zero APY. Read it from the chain for the deployed instance; do not infer it from documentation.

This report is written at the **armed** severity, deliberately. If the live `desiredAPYBps` is zero, the finding is **dormant — not invalid, and not Low**. A Low label would under-warn the owner at exactly the moment they later raise APY, which is precisely when the warning has value.

### Disclosure — this report overrides a standing ledger instruction

Two ledger artefacts carry an explicit escalation instruction on this class:

- **V2-M-03**'s `reclassNote`: *"RE-CLASSIFY HIGH under any non-zero-APY config."*
- **DEDUP-04**'s `reEmitTrigger`: *"RE-CLASSIFY HIGH and emit as a finding if any non-zero desiredAPYBps config is ever planned/deployed."*

**This report does not follow that instruction, and says so rather than quietly departing from it.** The escalation was written before the closed form was derived. It presumes the `S0/S1` factor is the impact. It is not: the closed form `excess == exitedAmount × APY × window / year` **contains no S1 term**, so the leak is emission-rate-limited and bounded by the protocol's pre-existing budget (above). At real parameters it is ~0.012%/day of supply. **High is not supportable even under a non-zero-APY config** — which is the exact condition the notes name.

**Recommended ledger amendment:** strike the `RE-CLASSIFY HIGH` language from V2-M-03's `reclassNote` and from DEDUP-04's `reEmitTrigger`, replacing it with *"emit at Medium under any non-zero-APY config; High is refuted by the closed form (no S1 term) — see pe9m2."* Left unamended, the next run will mechanically escalate to High and overstate the finding, which costs credibility on a report that is otherwise accurate. DEDUP-04's re-emit trigger itself should be **retained** — only its severity clause is wrong.

## Recommended mitigation steps

Call `_updatePhUSDEmissionRate()` in `pauseWithdraw` immediately after the `totalStaked` mutation at `:602`, matching the three other mutation sites:

```diff
     function pauseWithdraw(uint256 amount) external whenPaused {
         UserInfo storage user = userInfo[msg.sender];
         require(user.amount >= amount, "Insufficient balance");
         require(amount > 0, "Amount must be greater than 0");

         user.amount -= amount;
         totalStaked -= amount;
+        _updatePhUSDEmissionRate();

         user.phUSDDebt = (user.amount * accPhUSDPerShare) / PRECISION;
         user.stableDebt = (user.amount * accStablePerShare) / PRECISION;
         user.promoDebt = (user.amount * accPromoPerShare) / PRECISION;

         IERC20(address(phUSD)).safeTransfer(msg.sender, amount);

         emit EmergencyWithdrawal(msg.sender, amount);
     }
```

The negative control above measures this exact behaviour: with the rate re-derived at exit time, the survivor mints precisely their fair share and the excess over the APY budget is zero.

### ⚠ Remediation trap — fix the rate only, not the accounting

**Do not additionally add `_updatePool()` to the top of `pauseWithdraw`, and do not settle accrual inside it.**

`pauseWithdraw` is an ejector seat by design (story-022, *"recovery out-of-band"*); its docblock at [:586-591](https://github.com/Behodler/phlimbo-ea/blob/master/src/PhlimboV3.sol#L586-L591) records that the V3 debt realignment at `:604-606` exists specifically to fix the latent V1/V2 defect where a partial `pauseWithdraw` left debts computed against the old, larger amount, so `amount * acc - debt` underflowed and **bricked the user's position** after unpause. A naive settle-inside fix risks resurrecting that underflow brick. Ledger entry V3-Q-02 exists solely to carry this warning.

The correct fix is the **rate re-derivation only** — one line, no accounting change.

### Note for triage

This finding must not be collapsed into V3-Q-02 (`93cdca59`), which sits on the same contract and same function. V3-Q-02 is scoped to accounting that *exactly conserves* — "no insolvency, no over-emission". This finding is precisely the over-emission V3-Q-02 declares absent.
