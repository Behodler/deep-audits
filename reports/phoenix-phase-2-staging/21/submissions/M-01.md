## Description

`collectNudge` performs

```solidity
IERC20(token).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), amount);
s.buffer += amount;
```

at `NudgeStreamer.sol:149-150` with **no balance-delta measurement**. The credited buffer is the amount
*sent*, not the amount *received*.

The streamer holds a **single pooled balance per token** across every `(batchMinter, token)` pair, while
`_settle` caps payouts against the **per-stream** accounting buffer (`_accrued`, `:195-200`) rather than
against real custody. The contract NatSpec at `:192-194` asserts:

> The buffer cap guarantees the streamer can never transfer more than it holds, even if elapsed is large.

That guarantee is **per stream**, whereas custody is **pooled per token**, so it does not hold across
pairs.

> **Line-number note.** The finding record cites this NatSpec block variously as `:193-195` (in
> `severityRationale` and `likelihood`) and `:194-196` (in `description`), and `_accrued` as `:193-200`.
> Both are off by one to three lines. Re-verified this run against the file itself: the NatSpec block is
> `NudgeStreamer.sol:192-194` and `_accrued` is `:195-200`, identically at the nested pin
> (`d2506c19`) and at top-level `lib/phoenix-nft-staking` HEAD — the two are byte-identical
> (md5 `887e9ef9d92b551f25e7efc16e8d0dc5`). The citations used in this submission's own prose are the
> corrected ones; the quoted rationale above retains the record's original numbers. `:149-150`
> (`safeTransferFrom` + `s.buffer += amount`) is correct as recorded.

The deploy-script probe at `DeployMocks.s.sol:1661` is the only defence anywhere in the system, and it is
**vacuous**: it runs solely against `MockKendu` and `MockPhUSD`, both defined with no transfer fee, so it
cannot fail locally. There is **no on-chain guard**. `MockKendu`'s own NatSpec states the mock is
deliberately fee-free *because the streamer assumes exact delivery*.

Kendu Inu is a memecoin whose real transfer semantics are **unverified on-chain** — no read of
`0xaa95f26e30001251fb905d264aa7b00ee9df6c18` was performed this run (`review.md` §6, "What I could not
verify").

## Impact

For any reward token that is fee-on-transfer, taxed, or reflection-based:

1. The credited buffer immediately **exceeds custody**.
2. Whichever `(batchMinter, token)` pair settles first is paid its **full over-stated buffer** out of the
   shared balance, consuming the backing of the other pairs.
3. The later pair's `pullPendingStream` **reverts** on insufficient balance.
4. Because `BatchNFTMinterMultiToken.batchMint` loops `pullPendingStream` over `getNudgeTokens()` **inside
   one transaction** (`:539` at the pinned commit; `:530` at top-level HEAD), a single tainted token
   **bricks `batchMint` for ALL reward tokens** — the entire mint path, not just the tainted reward.

Two distinct effects, deliberately scored on the **availability** axis only:

- **Cross-pair consumption.** Pair A's settlement is paid out of the pooled per-token balance that also
  backs pair B. Because Phoenix nudge pots are funded by yield on protocol-owned capital, this is
  **misallocation between two legitimate recipient pots, not a value leak**, and is expressly not scored
  as economic loss.
- **Availability brick.** The under-backed pair's `pullPendingStream` reverts, and the one-transaction
  loop propagates that revert to the whole mint path. This is the **load-bearing impact** and it is a
  protocol-function failure.

No theft. No economic loss. No external beneficiary.

## Attack Path / Preconditions

1. Owner whitelists a fee-on-transfer / taxed / reflection token as a nudge token via
   `setNudgeTokenWhitelist` (non-obvious footgun; Kendu Inu's real transfer semantics are unverified
   on-chain).
2. A donor calls `collectNudge(batchMinter, token, amount)`; `NudgeStreamer.sol:149-150` does
   `safeTransferFrom` then `s.buffer += amount` with no balance-delta measurement, so the credited buffer
   exceeds actual custody by the tax.
3. A second `(batchMinter, token)` pair is registered on the **same** token. Custody is pooled per token;
   the cap in `_accrued` is per **stream**, so total credited buffers now exceed the single pooled
   balance.
4. Whichever pair settles first is paid its full over-stated buffer out of the shared balance, consuming
   the other pair's backing.
5. The later pair's `pullPendingStream` reverts on insufficient balance.
6. `batchMint`, which loops `pullPendingStream` over all whitelisted tokens in one tx, now reverts for
   **all** reward tokens — the whole mint path is down, not just the tainted reward.

**Assumptions**

1. A fee-on-transfer / taxed / reflection token is whitelisted as a nudge token.
2. For the cross-pair drain specifically, at least two `(batchMinter, token)` pairs share one token; the
   single-pair case yields under-delivery and a stuck residual rather than a revert.
3. Fee-on-transfer is **in scope** for this family (story-022 §4.4).

**External requirements.** The owner must admit a taxed token through `setNudgeTokenWhitelist`. No
timing, MEV, price, or third-party dependency.

**Likelihood.** Medium-low as a trigger, high as a consequence once triggered. There is **no external
attacker and no unauthorised actor** anywhere in the path.

## Recommended Mitigation

> Measure the receipt in `collectNudge`:
>
> ```solidity
> uint256 before = IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this));
> safeTransferFrom(...);
> uint256 received = IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this)) - before;
> s.buffer += received;
> ```
>
> and recompute the rate from `received`.
>
> If fee-on-transfer tokens are intended to be permanently out of scope, add an on-chain probe in
> `registerStream` (which is already owner-gated and is the natural admission point) rather than relying
> on a deploy-script assert against a mock that cannot fail.
>
> Independently, verify the real Kendu token's transfer semantics on-chain BEFORE
> `setNudgeTokenWhitelist` on mainnet.

**Blocking pre-broadcast action** (carried on the L-09 reopen trigger): tick story 072 Preflight line 514
against the **real** Kendu token `0xaa95f26e30001251fb905d264aa7b00ee9df6c18`. Story 073's `MockKendu`
probe does **not** discharge it.

Secondary, non-blocking: the NatSpec at `NudgeStreamer.sol:192-194` should be corrected — as written it
asserts a cross-pair guarantee the code does not provide, and it is the reason an operator would never
think to check.
