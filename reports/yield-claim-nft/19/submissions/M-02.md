# [M-02] WITHDRAWN — the Medium rested on a stranding claim that is refuted by mint atomicity

**This finding is withdrawn as a Medium.** It is re-severed **Medium → Low** and **folded into QA `L-01`** (`reports/yield-claim-nft/19/submissions/qa-report.md`, ledger entry **L-16** / `b0aa0f58…`). No separate submission survives; this file is kept so the retraction is visible rather than silent (Law 1: park it in a channel someone reads, never drop it).

The label `M-02` is **retired, not reused**. No other label was renumbered.

## 1. What the withdrawn finding claimed

That story-046's mandatory `NudgeStreamer` leg, combined with `NudgeRatchet`'s lack of a `rescueERC20`, leaves **USDC resident on the dispatcher unreachable by any actor including the owner** — and that this stranding, not the availability wedge, carried it to Medium.

## 2. Why that is wrong

**The mint is atomic.** `NFTMinterV2._executeMint` performs the user's `safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, dispatcher, price)` and the `dispatch(...)` call **in the same transaction** (`src/NFTMinterV2.sol:181-190`). If the streamer leg reverts, the inbound transfer reverts with it. **No user payment can ever become resident on `NudgeRatchet`.** The withdrawn finding treated the mint as if the payment landed first and the forwarding failed afterwards; it does not.

Nor can a *successful* dispatch leave a remainder. `_dispatch` approves and hands over the **full balance** (`bal`), and `NudgeStreamer.collectNudge` pulls exactly that amount in one `safeTransferFrom` (`NudgeStreamer.sol:149`) — no partial pull, no fee-on-transfer (USDC), no dust branch. A successful dispatch always leaves the ratchet at zero.

Exhaustively, the ways USDC can be resident on `NudgeRatchet`:

| Path | Real? |
|---|---|
| A user mint whose dispatch reverted | **No** — atomic revert, payment never lands |
| A successful dispatch leaving a remainder | **No** — full-balance sweep, exact-amount pull |
| Direct transfer / airdrop / mis-send / ops pre-funding | **Yes — and it is the only one** |

So the value at risk is **exclusively out-of-band strays**. That is precisely what ledger entry **L-08** described when it was filed, and L-08 was correctly a **Low**.

**And it is not even permanently stranded under the load-bearing trigger.** The withdrawn finding's severity rested on the live-repoint sub-case of trigger B — an owner `setBatchMinter` onto an unregistered pair. That is curable by the owner (`registerStream` the new pair, or repoint back), and the very next dispatch sweeps everything including the strays. Temporarily-unsweepable strays plus an availability wedge is **exactly QA `L-01`**, at Low.

Permanent unreachability required trigger **C** (USDC blacklists the streamer) — which the submission itself declared explicitly non-load-bearing. It cannot be simultaneously excluded from the severity argument and relied on to produce the irreversibility that justifies it.

## 3. The PoC is sound; its interpretation was not

`reports/yield-claim-nft/19/pocs/run19-Tier3Nudge.patch` (`Run19_T2_RatchetWedge`, 4/4 pass) is not retracted and nothing in it was mis-measured:

- `test_T2a` / `test_T2b` genuinely prove the wedge — that substance now lives in QA `L-01`.
- `test_T2c` genuinely proves `rescueERC20` is absent (with a working positive control on `NudgeRatchetDelayRelease`). What it does **not** prove is that meaningful value can be sitting there: the 500 USDC it strands is *placed by the test harness*, i.e. the out-of-band path, not a path any user mint can produce.
- `test_T2d` genuinely proves the shared-streamer blacklist tail. See §5 — that is accepted, not filed.

## 4. Ledger disposition

- **L-08 (`0b97f155…`) — reopen DECLINED; stays `fixed`.** The story-038 full-balance sweep is intact in source and correct. The "expired closure" argument was that story-046 destroyed the self-cleaning property the closure rested on; in fact the sweep still self-cleans on every successful dispatch, and the only interruption is the curable wedge. The closure rationale ("judge on whether out-of-band USDC becomes recoverable") still holds.
- **The wedge half is not erased.** It was already filed independently as ledger **L-16** (`b0aa0f58…`) = QA `L-01`, open, Low. The contingency fingerprint `03864c76…` reserved in L-08's run-19 note is therefore **not minted** — it would be a duplicate of L-16.
- **`L-16`'s `distinctFrom` clause is amended.** It previously kept L-16 separate from L-08 on the grounds that "on `NudgeRatchet` it additionally strands funds with no rescue path at all, which is a different impact requiring a different mitigation." That justification is withdrawn by §2. The `NudgeRatchet`-specific rider (no `rescueERC20`, so strays are unsweepable for the duration of the wedge) is now carried **inside** `L-01`.

## 5. Owner decision on the recommendations (2026-07-25)

The withdrawn submission asked for three things. All are declined on a single basis: **`NudgeStreamer`'s liveness and registration promises are universal** — every donor dispatcher in the topology depends on the streamer being live and on its `(batchMinter, token)` pair being registered. That is an accepted protocol-wide assumption, not a `NudgeRatchet`-specific defect, so:

1. A `NudgeRatchet`-specific `rescueERC20` — **declined.** The sweep already recovers strays once the universal precondition holds.
2. A `donationEnabled` / `try-catch` degraded mode on `NudgeRatchet` — **declined.** The mandatory-streamer coupling is the deliberate story-046 design.
3. An owner rescue on `NudgeStreamer` — **not filed.** Same basis; and the streamer is a `phoenix-nft-staking` contract, so a first-party `yield-claim-nft` finding is the wrong home for it.

What remains reportable is unchanged and is carried by QA `L-01`: the **repoint** sub-case is a genuine Law-3 footgun (silent arming, cure in another repository), at Low, with the atomic-repoint guard as its recommendation.

**Re-open trigger (single, specific):** if a future change makes the user's payment land on `NudgeRatchet` **non-atomically** with `dispatch` — a queued/deferred dispatch, a two-step mint, or any path that credits the dispatcher without forwarding in the same transaction — §2 collapses and the Medium becomes correct as originally written. Nothing at `d4cc563` does this.
