WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
---------------------
M-07 claims the `minPhUSDOut` slippage floor at src/Antimatter.sol:260 is inert
for every floor in [0, amount], and that `amount + 1` is therefore the first
floor that can ever bind. A finding of that shape lives or dies on its control:
a guard that never fires is indistinguishable from a harness that never reaches
the guard, so the control must be shown to be genuinely load-bearing rather than
merely green. These four runs show exactly that. Mutating either argument of the
expected `InsufficientPhUSDOut(minPhUSDOut, received)` revert data, or its
selector, turns the passing control red — so the assertion is matching the exact
revert payload byte for byte, not merely observing that "something reverted".
Mutating the floor down by one wei, from `amount + 1` to `amount`, turns it red
with "next call did not revert as expected" — so `amount + 1` is demonstrably
the boundary: one wei lower and the guard cannot fire at all. That boundary is
precisely the top edge of the inert band the finding asserts, established
empirically rather than by reading the source. The unmutated control passes,
so the red results are attributable to the mutations and to nothing else.
