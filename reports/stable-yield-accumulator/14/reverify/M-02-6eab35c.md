    (uint256 duration,,,) = INudgeStreamerStreams(nudgeStreamer).streams(nudge, _rewardToken);

## 5. Classification — LIKELY-FIXED (filed root-cause path)

The filed attack step was: **a single `setRewardToken` call on an armed nudge rail silently re-keys
the stream, and the brick only surfaces later at `claim()`**. At `6eab35c` that exact step is
**rejected before any state is written** — the transaction reverts inside `setRewardToken`, so the
silent-re-key-then-later-brick sequence no longer exists. The error moves from a deferred, opaque
`claim()`-time failure to an immediate, named, configuration-time rejection, which is exactly the
remediation the finding asked for.

**LIKELY-FIXED, not FIXED.** Per the standing rule, a fix that merely stops tripping the harness is
not a verified fix. The status stays `fix-pending`; only a human closes it.

## 6. ⚠ RESIDUAL PATHS — the brick is still constructible

**This section is the reason closure is not recommended unconditionally.**

The guard is **one-directional**: it validates the *token* being set, on the *one* setter that sets
it, and only while the split is armed. The fix's own NatSpec at `src/StableYieldAccumulator.sol:446-449`
**admits** all three residual paths. In-source NatSpec carries **no suppression authority** — a
documented residual path is still a live path when it reproduces, and these reproduce.

All three were probed empirically in `workspace/stable-yield-accumulator/test/recheck-M02-residual-6eab35c.t.sol`
(**3/3 pass**, each with its own positive-control claim and abort-on-empty tripwire). **All three
reach the identical bricked end state** — `claim()` reverting:

```
0xd62cbfe8  NudgeStreamer__NotRegistered()
```

| # | Path | Guard coverage | Reproduces | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **a1** | `setNudgeAddress` (`:485`) | **NONE** — no guard at all, not even a zero-address check | **REACHABLE** | One owner call re-keys to `streams[newSink][reward]` with `duration == 0`. **Non-transient**: still `0xd62cbfe8` after **+365 days**. |
| **a2** | `setNudgeStreamer` (`:513`) | zero-address check only | **REACHABLE** | Repointing to a streamer that never ran `registerStream` gives the identical brick. |
| **b** | `setNudgeSplit(0)` → `setRewardToken(newToken)` → `setNudgeSplit(50)` | bypassed — guard is conditional on `nudgeSplit != 0` | **REACHABLE** | Restores the exact pre-fix bricked state, with the new token otherwise fully configured. A three-call sequence with no warning at any step. |

**a1 and a2 were each preceded by a legitimate, guard-passing `setRewardToken` call**, confirming the
guard was genuinely armed and was not incidentally bypassed by an unconfigured fixture.

Net: `setRewardToken` — the single doorway the fix closed — is no longer a route to the brick, but the
**availability outage the Medium was ranked on remains constructible** via three other owner
operations, one of which (`a1`) needs a single call and is completely unguarded.
